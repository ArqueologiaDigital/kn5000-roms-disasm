# Lane promB4 — prom_b's five high-region `.incbin` spans, 2026-09-02

QUESTION ANSWERED: what is inside `wsa1/original_ROMs/wsa1_prom_b.ic13` at
`0x0396E7+70`, `0x03A0E9+230`, `0x03C47E+87`, `0x03DA77+393` and
`0x03DCBF+359` — 1,139 B of the image's 10,664 B of verbatim debt — and what
evidence licenses converting it?

Answer: **all five are interpreter-B display-list RECORDS and the fixed-stride
OPERAND ARRAYS those records' `+0x07` pointers name.** Everything is emitted as
typed data (`.byte` / `.short` / `.long` with a stated record shape). **Nothing
in this lane was disassembled into instructions**, because nothing in it is
code: every reference to these bytes loads their ADDRESS; none calls or
branches to them.

## How each object is licensed

Two rules, both already used elsewhere in this tree, applied by
`wsa1/notes/gen_prom_b_promB4_operand_arrays.py --check`:

* **records** — the `(op, length)` pair must satisfy **exactly one** of the two
  interpreters' implied-length rules, read from the ROM's own handler tables
  `HTBL_A` (`0xF31D21`) / `HTBL_B` (`0xF31DB1`). Never both, never neither.
  This is `notes/gen_prom_b_tiny_records_batch.py`'s rule.
* **arrays** — a record's `+0x07` pointer lands on the first byte; the HANDLER
  fixes the entry width (`0xF31B57` `sla 3,HL` ⇒ 8 B, `0xF31B86` `mul HL,6` ⇒
  6 B, `0xF31B21` takes it from the record's own `+0x0B`); the extent is a
  whole number of entries; and the extent ends on a boundary established
  **outside the span**. This is `notes/prom_b_dl_operand_tables.py`'s rule.

⚠ `(mask >> shift) + 1` is an upper bound on the INDEX, never the size. It
happens to equal the extent exactly once here (`0xF3DA77`: `(0x0C >> 2) + 1 =
4`, and the array is 4 entries) and that is recorded as a bonus, not as the
source of the number.

### The part that is not self-referential

Four record starts are named from OUTSIDE prom_b's own data, by code that loads
them into `XIY` and calls an interpreter-B handler directly through a thunk:

| site | loads | then calls | resolves to |
|---|---|---|---|
| prom_b `0xF7E966` | `0x00F396E2` | `T_F4181C` | `jp 0xF31B57` (op 03/08, 8-byte entries) |
| prom_a `0xF81E93`, `0xF81EE3` | `0x00F3DA77` | `T_F4181C` | same |
| prom_a `0xF81EAE` | `0x00F3DAA2` | `T_F4181C` | same |
| prom_a `0xF81ECC` | `0x00F3DAED` | `T_F4181C` | same |

`0xF396E2` is the load-bearing one: it proves the 398-byte extent of
`Data_F39559` had swallowed the first five bytes of a record.

## Four headers overturned — and rewritten, not deleted

Every one of them had already warned "⚠ the extent is the reachability walk's,
not the object's". Following that warning is what found these objects.

| header | said | is |
|---|---:|---:|
| `Data_F39559` | 398 B | 393 B |
| `Data_F3A0E4` | 5 B | 11 B |
| `Data_F3C37D` | 257 B | 48 B — it ran through a whole SECOND array and one byte into its 27th entry |
| `Data_F3DC21` | 158 B | 154 B |

The four enclosing `COVER-R1` blocks also carried "everything else here is NOT
reachable and stays `.incbin`". That verdict was true of the round-1
reachability WALK and false of the ROM: these arrays are reached by pointer
from records the walk never enters, because nothing CALLS them. The verdict is
left standing with the correction underneath it.

## The one weaker claim, stated as such

Span 5's 24 records are reached by **no call site this tree knows**, and no
32-bit word anywhere in the four ROMs names `0xF3DCBB`. Their framing rests on
a two-sided argument instead: walking 15-byte `op 02` records BACKWARD from
`0xF3DE23` — an address three other records (`0xF3D3C3`, `0xF3D3E1`,
`0xF3D3FF`) already point at — lands on `0xF3DCBB` after exactly 24 records and
no further, the byte before it being `0x2A`, past the `0x24` opcode bound. The
run is maximal and self-terminating; the 24 records are byte-for-byte identical
but for their source variable (`0x2658..0x265F`, `0x1301..0x1310`) and screen
position (`0x0A51`/`0x1159`/`0x1861` + 4k), three clean arithmetic runs of
eight. Strong, but weaker than the other four spans, and recorded as such in
the source header too.

## Two of `prom_b_dl_operand_tables.py`'s extents are refined, not contradicted

That tool reads the ROM, not the source, so its output is unchanged by this
lane — but two of its candidate extents run past the object they measure,
because it stops only at the next thing it already knows about:

* gap `0xF39559-0xF3972C`: it reports `0xF39602 + 299 B` (42 × 7). The string
  table is **32 entries × 7 = 224 B**, ending at `0xF396E2` where the record
  this lane converted begins.
* gap `0xF3DC21-0xF3DE25`: it reports `0xF3DC5D + 457 B` (76 × 6). That array
  is **5 entries × 6 = 30 B**; the remaining 427 B are a 64-byte 1-byte-entry
  table, 24 op-02 records, and the 3-byte table at `0xF3DE23`.

Both are the tool behaving as documented — it calls these extents CANDIDATES
until a partition test confirms them, and neither gap passed that test. Noted
here so the next reader does not quote the 299 and the 457 as measurements.
