WHAT REPRODUCES 3 OF 3
  * ADD and HOLD both create register C3 = 1259EA; the shipped alias NEVER does.
  * ADD alone perturbs C8 (C46363); CONTROL and HOLD both leave it FFFFFF.
  * the arm fires identically across the three masks within each program (reach is not the variable).

WHAT DOES *NOT* REPRODUCE -- and it corrects the first write-up
  * "the control is the only arm leaving D0 unrailed" holds on TYPE 33 and 34 only.
    On TYPE 35 the CONTROL ALREADY RAILS D0 (7FFFFF), so railing cannot be the discriminator.
  * 0E exists as a non-zero cell only in prog75, so its railing is a one-program observation.
