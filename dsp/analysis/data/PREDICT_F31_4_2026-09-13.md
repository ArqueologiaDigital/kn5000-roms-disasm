## ★ RESULT, 2026-09-14 — **N0 PASS, N4 REFUTED, N1 AND N2 REFUTED.** The shipped alias survives.
Data: [`f31_4_run_2026-09-14.txt`](f31_4_run_2026-09-14.txt). TYPE 33 = `prog75_peq_compressor`,
**fingerprinted** (`type_fingerprint.py` ✅, the §193 obligation).

```
   mask / reading                     cells   0E        C3        C8        D0
   CONTROL  4 -> LOAD (shipped alias) 72      7B3F32    --        FFFFFF    62FFEE
   ARM      4 = ADD                   73      7FFFFF    1259EA    C46363    7FFFFF
   ARM      4 = HOLD                  73      7FFFFF    1259EA    FFFFFF    7FFFFF
```

* **N0 REACH — PASS.** `§205 f31=4` fired **1 387 425**. §108 measured **0** in the chorus, so this
  run has power where that one did not.
* **N4 (the pre-registered null) — REFUTED.** The three arms are **not** bit-identical: they differ
  in the §160 register file, in §167's tempB change count, in §169's product and in §174's operand.
  The word's accumulator **is** observed, so the blindness lemma does not apply here.
* **N1 and N2 — REFUTED, and this is the directional result.** I predicted ADD would leave the
  anchored cells at least as healthy and the LOAD alias would degrade the band. The opposite:
  **ADD and HOLD both drive `0E` and `D0` to `7FFFFF`, the positive rail, and both add a cell
  `C3`. The shipped `4 → LOAD` alias is the only arm that leaves those two unrailed.**
* **ADD vs HOLD are nearly degenerate** — they differ only at `C8` (`C46363` vs `FFFFFF`) and by
  24 in one operand-change count, so this run does not separate them from each other.

### ⚠ And the criteria I pre-registered were the wrong ones
`N1`'s instruments — hand-off `0x05` live, VOLUME `0x06` written-once — are **identical to the
digit in all three arms**. They cannot see this code at all. What separates the arms is the §160
register-file census, which was **not** among the pre-registered criteria. The run is still valid
(the discriminator was recorded by the device either way), but the lesson is that the criterion
list was chosen from the *previous* investigation's vocabulary rather than from this word's
position.

### What it does and does not establish
⇒ **The corpus and the machine disagree.** §144's static argument favoured ADD (22 of 25 words at
band slot 2 carry `mac`); the machine disfavours it (ADD rails two registers the shipped alias does
not). That is a real tension and it is the first machine evidence on `f31 = 4` in this project.
⛔ It does **not** decode the code: "the shipped alias survives where two rivals rail" is
elimination pressure on ADD and HOLD, not a positive reading of 4, and railing being *wrong* is an
inference (a strong one here — §35 disqualified SPEC bit 28 on exactly that basis).
⛔ TYPE 34 / 35 (algos 96 / 97) are **not yet run**; §111's boundary says one program's result does
not generalise.
