  op 0x72 -- the compressor's GAIN COMPUTER, the dynamics family's only PROVEN
  cell<-opcode entry.  Evaluator 0x039ABD; ROM data 0x012DB3..0x012E03 (80 bytes).
================================================================================================

   ★ DOUBLES (IEEE-754, little-endian -- the CPU's byte order)

      012DB3  B9 FC 87 F4 DB D7 B1 BF   = -0.0697
      012DBB  00 00 00 00 00 00 24 40   = 10
      012DC3  1E A7 E8 48 2E FF EF 3F   = 0.9999
      012DCB  B9 FC 87 F4 DB D7 B1 BF   = -0.0697
      012DD3  00 00 00 00 00 00 24 40   = 10
      012DDB  1E A7 E8 48 2E FF EF 3F   = 0.9999

      the two halves are byte-identical: ★ YES -- one law, two operands
      (and `op0x72` writes exactly TWO cells in every carrier: prog36 0x04/0x0D,
       prog75 0x0A/0x19 -- so `two operands' is what the cell map already said.)

   ★ FLOATS (IEEE-754, little-endian)

      012DE3  00 00 00 40   = 2             
      012DE7  00 00 00 40   = 2             
      012DEB  00 00 80 3F   = 1             
      012DEF  00 00 40 40   = 3             
      012DF3  00 00 00 4A   = 2097152          ★ = 2^21
      012DF7  00 00 00 4B   = 8388608          ★ = 2^23
      012DFB  00 00 80 4A   = 4194304          ★ = 2^22
      012DFF  00 00 00 4A   = 2097152          ★ = 2^21

   ⇒ FOUR DISTINCT POWER-OF-TWO SCALES, written down in the ROM.
     sect. 140 stopped because *"the per-coefficient scales are solved for the EQ
     only"* -- the EQ's came from a flatness IDENTITY this family has no analogue of.
     Here they need no solving at all.

   ⛔ The constants are NOT the law.  How they combine is in the evaluator's 203
     lines of TLCS-900, which this file does not read, so no formula is claimed.
