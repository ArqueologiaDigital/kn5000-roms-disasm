## Why these two, and why now
`f31` 5 (42 sole / 55) and `f31` 4 (34 / 46) are the third and fourth entries in the queue.
`f31-high.md` item I: *"Four readings, no discriminator run against known mathematics yet. All 203
words keep trapping."* §106 supplied the missing ingredient — **criteria the ROM and the FIRMWARE
state**, each demonstrably able to fail:

* the **VOLUME** cell `0x06` — `register-space.md` item A1, PROVEN BY CONSTRUCTION in 49 of 49
  algorithms, written once by `EFF_VolumeLoop`. Under a wrong accumulator model it rails and churns
  (measured: 5 of 5 out-of-sample, 11 … 177 316 changes).
* the **LFO** — `lfo-ramp.md` anchors 29 blocks nine-fold; a wrong model kills the phase cell or
  runs it at an absurd rate (measured: `clr:after` dead, `LD@before` a 4.0-frame period).

And the arms already exist: `m_bx_f4` (SPEC bits 48-49) and `m_bx_f5` (bits 50-51), readings
`1 = LOAD`, `2 = ADD`, `3 = HOLD`; `0` keeps the shipped alias `op = f31 & 3`, i.e. **4 → LOAD** and
**5 → ADD**. ★ The kernel's `w30` carries `f31 == 5`, so the code runs in **every frame of every
program** — the reach test is structural before it is measured.
