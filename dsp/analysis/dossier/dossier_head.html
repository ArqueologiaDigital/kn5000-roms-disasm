What this is. A complete account of what is currently known about the reverb effect of the Technics SX-KN5000 keyboard, as implemented on its undocumented NEC uPD6383GF signal processor, together with the kernel — the fixed preamble that runs on every frame of every preset and which turns out to compute using the reverb's own coefficients.

It assumes no prior knowledge of the instrument, the chip or the project. Every substantive claim carries an evidence grade. The plumbing is largely known; the transfer function is not — and roughly three fifths of the instruction words in the reverb image cannot yet be decoded at all.
