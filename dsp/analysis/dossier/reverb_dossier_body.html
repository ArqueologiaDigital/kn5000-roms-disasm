The reverb of the Technics SX-KN5000

A reverse-engineering dossier on one 133-word microprogram for an undocumented effects DSP — what has been measured, what has been forced, what is guessed, and what is still unreadable. About a quarter of the program has no decoded meaning at all, and the emulation of it is silent.

1. What this document covers

The Technics SX-KN5000 is a 1996 arranger keyboard. Its audio path ends in two digital effects processors: IC311, an NEC uPD6383GF, and IC310, an MN19413. Neither has a public datasheet — no instruction set, no register map, no pin description. Everything below about how IC311 executes was derived from the machine’s own firmware, from the microprograms that firmware uploads, and from a behavioural emulation written against them.

IC311 is a two-unit machine: it runs two programs per audio frame out of one 384-word instruction RAM, unit 0 from I-RAM 84 and unit 1 from I-RAM 200. The front panel exposes exactly that split. Its DSP EFFECT page drives unit 0 (chorus, flanger, rotary speaker, distortion, parametric EQ…); its DIGITAL REVERB page drives unit 1. The reverb is unit 1. MEASURED — the ROM’s algorithm table, both front-panel lists (byte-identical ROM tables at fixed file offsets in the main program ROM) and the load base of every uploaded image agree.

The DIGITAL REVERB page offers fourteen entries. Twelve — ROOM 1/2, PLATE 1/2, CONCERT 1/2, DARK 1/2, BRIGHT 1/2, WAVE 1/2 — are reverb tanks and are this document’s subject. The last two, SINGLE DELAY and MULTI TAP DELAY, are unit-0 delay programs re-offered on the same page and are not reverbs. MEASURED (the page’s forward table and its inverse resolve to algorithms [9, 10, 16…27]). The twelve tanks share one microprogram: all twelve algorithm-table slots hold the identical stream pointer, and that stream carries one instruction-RAM record — 133 words at I-RAM 200 — and no coefficient records. MEASURED Everything that distinguishes the presets is data.

1.1 How much is actually known

Enough to draw the reverb’s plumbing and not enough to write down its transfer function. Measured or forced: the delay memory, the eleven delay lines and their exact lengths in samples, the pre-delay buffer and its seven taps, which coefficient cell every multiply consumes, which front-panel control writes which cell, and the exact arithmetic by which the firmware turns a REVERB TIME in seconds into a loop gain. Not known: what the eight-word block that repeats nine times actually computes. An exhaustive search says one-multiplier all-pass in one model of the machine and says comb-family — specifically not all-pass — in another, and in the operating regime the rest of the program demands, no reference topology survives at all. Thirty-two of the 133 words carry no decoded reading, and one of them is the single word that carries the reverb’s result out of the body. The emulation is digitally silent: 1 106 028 executions of the unit-1 output stage produced zero non-zero samples and a peak of 0.

Before quoting anything below. Numbers are given with populations and denominators because several have been wrong in this project’s own notes. The frame clock is 44 100 Hz; any millisecond figure from a log written before that was established is 8.84 % too high (those logs divided a frame count by 44 100 while the emulated clock ran at 48 000). Every millisecond figure here is computed at 44 100 Hz. Frame counts from before the correction are not comparable with counts from after it — only ratios are.

2. How to read the evidence grades

Every substantive claim carries one of four grades. They are not degrees of confidence; they name the kind of evidence.

MEASURED
Read directly out of the ROM, out of the 3057-word microprogram corpus, out of an archived capture of the wire between the Sub CPU and the DSP, or out of an instrumented emulation run. A measurement can still measure the wrong thing; where that risk is live it is stated.
FORCED
Every survivor of an exhaustive enumeration agrees. Example: that addr8 bit 6 is the delay-port direction field — over 133 decidable same-value word pairs exactly one of the 36 bits reaches zero violations, runner-up five.
INFERRED
Reasonable and consistent with everything known, but not compelled. Anything graded this way could be wrong without contradicting a measurement.
SPECULATIVE
A reading with no discriminating evidence. Very little here is graded this way, because such readings were dropped rather than printed.

A fifth word, UNDECODED, marks words with no reading at all. When an undecoded region is described, the grade attaches to the observation that the words are undecoded — a measurement — never to any account of what they compute.

2.1 The instruction word

An IC311 instruction is 36 bits: hi12[35:24] . class4[23:20] . addr8[19:12] . lo12[11:0]. Within lo12, SRC = lo12[10:6] selects the operand route and ACT = lo12[4:0] the action. Within hi12, bit 11 is a format escape, bit 10 END-of-block, bit 4 a store, bits [9:8] a field called f98 and bits [3:1] one called f31. A word with hi12[11:8] == 0xC is in a different “C” format where those bit meanings do not apply. MEASURED

Two escape-conditional traps, both of which have produced wrong published counts. END is meaningful only when the escape is clear: corpus-wide 121 words carry raw bit 10, only 52 are real ENDs. The store bit is likewise gated: 708 raw, 704 rendered. In the reverb image five words carry raw bit 10 — the four C-format words and w132 — and exactly one, w132, is a real END; all 23 store words are escape-clear, so the store count of 23 stands. MEASURED Related terminology trap: in C-format hi12[11:8] is a format tag, not an escape flag.

2.2 Decode coverage — the single most important number here

Decode coverage over the whole corpus is 38.5 % — 1178 of 3057 words on the project’s alu_decoded() predicate (“the ALU operation of this word is decoded”). MEASURED A second predicate, decoded(), which also admits the nop special case, gives 1247 of 3057 = 40.8 %. Both are published side by side; quoting one without saying which is quoting an ambiguous number. The population is the 40 word-bearing disassembly listings (41 files, one an index with no words): 38 distinct IC311 body images totalling 2974 words, plus a 60-word kernel header and a 23-word epilogue.

A large part of the listing in section 7 is therefore undecoded, and that is expected. On the same ALU predicate the reverb image is 50 of 133 = 37.6 % decoded — slightly below the corpus mean. On the tier-1 predicate it is 69 of 133 = 51.9 %; adding tier 2 (words whose operation is known but whose operand encoding is not: the 28 delay-DRAM port words and the 4 C-format words) reaches 101 of 133 = 75.9 %. What a reader of the committed disassembly actually sees is starker: 64 of 133 rows render as ?word, 48.1 % of the listing. Thirty-two words have no reading of any kind. MEASURED

Coefficients. Each preset uploads 37 cells into C-RAM[0x90..0xB4], in two runs (30 from base 0x90, then 7 from 0xAE); BRIGHT REVERB 2 uploads 38, to 0xB5, its whole bank shifted by one. MEASURED Switching CONCERT REVERB 1 → ROOM REVERB 1 rewrites 23 of those cells in the canned block and touches nothing else in the 256-cell coefficient RAM. MEASURED Two of the 23 (0x9E, 0xA6) are immediately overwritten by a runtime poke that lands on the same value in both presets, so the number of chip-visible differences is 21. 4. Signal flow

The 133 words decompose exactly as B A&sup5; B A⁴ B C C: three copies of a ten-word separator block (B), nine copies of an eight-word ladder stage (A) and two copies of an eleven-word output tail (C). Every one of those eleven blocks owns exactly one delay-DRAM write and exactly one delay-DRAM read. MEASURED

Before the first B is a head: a delay read (the pre-delay tap), a load that is presumably the audio input, three coefficient multiplies, two undecoded words, a four-word damping unit, a “prime” delay write, a tap gain, four more words of which two are undecoded, a delay read, and the decay multiply. The head is not arbitrary: w7…w17 is block B rotated by six with its two anchored carry-copies replaced by null-action words, matching at 9 of 11 positions — the literal signature of a software-pipelined loop prologue. MEASURED

The pairing of writes to reads follows a fixed rule: block j writes line j−1 and reads line j+1 (a trail of two port slots). So the line a block writes is read by the block two repetitions earlier in program order — at the port level, a recirculation. MEASURED Whether it is an audio recirculation depends on what the blocks compute, which is not known. The eleven lines abut: the read address of line k equals the base of line k+1, so the tank is one contiguous region; for ROOM REVERB 1 it spans 41 590 to 45 463, 3873 samples = 87.82 ms. MEASURED

One further buffer sits outside the chain. Descriptor cell 0x03 = 32768 — exactly unit 1’s floor — is its base and seven taps read off it: the pre-delay tap at w0, three left-channel early reflections and three right-channel ones. Its write is w19, the first ladder repetition’s own slot 0. MEASURED Four of the seven taps are reached by the C-format words, whose direction still traps in the disassembler; those four rest on a consumer-counting argument (without them the last three class-1 words shift back four cells and the ceiling cell is never walked, 12 of 12 presets) rather than on the direction rule. The cross-channel ratio of the first left and right taps is 1.2006 ± 0.0032 across twelve independently canned presets — one design quantity, the left/right decorrelation. MEASURED

4.1 What the ladder stage computes — the central open question

Not known, and the reason no impulse response can be predicted from the ROM. Two searches disagree.
  • An exhaustive search in a four-register model of the machine forces a software-pipelined one-multiplier all-pass: 36 machines survive, all reproducing a textbook all-pass cascade at maximum coefficient error 0, in two role families the numbers cannot separate.
  • Re-run in the device’s own ACTION/SRC vocabulary, the first-order all-pass scores zero survivors — as do the parallel comb bank, Moorer, the nested all-pass and the Gray-Markel lattice, including in a topology-neutral pool — while pipe-comb (335) and a series comb cascade (208 of 400 sampled from an exhaustive 82 632) score.
  • But every survivor requires the blocking read latency, which the output tail falsifies 24 of 24 versus 0 of 24 on the disagreement sites. In the read regime the rest of the program requires, the search returns 0 of 400 under all three drain conventions.
Comb-family is therefore CONSISTENT, not FORCED, and “all-pass diffuser” — what this project’s own program manifest still calls it, at confidence SOLVED — is not supported in the device’s own vocabulary. MEASURED as a statement about the searches; the topology itself is UNDECODED. One weak corpus tie-break exists: the write-data-source question ranks role family B first on a 44-of-44 arm. That ranks; it does not decide.

5. The delay memory and the parameter path

5.1 The memory

The external delay store is IC309, a Mitsubishi M5M44260AJ-7S MEASURED (service manual; independently confirmed by the owner of a real instrument). That it is a 4 Mbit × 16 (256K × 16) DRAM is INFERRED from the part number and standard practice — no datasheet has been in hand. Only A0–A8 reach the part, A9–A16 unconnected MEASURED; whether that means nine-bit row/column multiplexing or a genuinely nine-bit bus is INFERRED. The DSP’s own external-RAM straps MD1–MD4 (IC311 pins 32–35) are all tied to VDD = 0b1111 MEASURED (service manual p. 28 / schematic p. 35, owner-verified); what that encoding selects — in particular whether the DSP emits 8 or 9 column bits, hence 17 or 18 address bits — is a uPD6383 question no document answers. The modulus N in “address = cell + G mod 2N” is therefore open between 16, 17 and 18, and it is provably unobservable from software: the firmware never emits an address above 64 899. The emulator stores the top 16 of 24 bits per delay word; that the store really is 16 bits wide has independent support — the block diagram of the other product built around this DSP, the Pioneer CDJ-500, shows its delay memory reached through I/O1–16 against a 24-bit core INFERRED (which 16 remains open).

5.3 The parameter path

The Sub CPU talks to IC311 over a small command space: command 0x01 to word address 0x0160 is a poke port, command 0x02 with prefix 0x0161 a 24-bit bulk coefficient port. In neither case is the destination in the value packet — an ldptr instruction word goes through the poke port first (801.0.NN.821 for coefficient RAM, 801.0.NN.825 for the descriptor bank, a class-1 form for the register file), then values stream with auto-increment. MEASURED The same 24-bit value has two packings: path A (bulk) carries three raw bytes; path B (a five-byte poke) carries 7+8+8+1 bits, i.e. twice the three raw bytes plus the tag byte’s bit 7. A tag routes the poke — 0x15 register file, 0x26 coefficient RAM, 0x4C descriptor bank — and there is no host path into the pointer-walked data RAM at all. FORCED (the two firmware writers differ in exactly one instruction, an immediate 0x26 against 0x4C). Positive control for the doubling rule: in the same capture the poke 0A 11 80 0F 26 into cell 0x9E decodes to 0x23001E, byte-identical to CONCERT REVERB 1’s canned value; a wrong payload rule cannot land on a canned cell exactly. MEASURED

A preset change is therefore 30 coefficient cells from 0x90, 7 from 0xAE, 30 descriptor cells from 0x00 and 2 from 0x1E — then runtime pokes, which are not incidental: ten coefficient cells are re-poked on every preset step (90 97 9E 9F A0 A6 A7 A8 AC B2), and one descriptor cell too. MEASURED

The famous 800-sample pre-delay is a canned-bank number, not a wire number. Replaying the tag-0x4C stream out of the archived captures, the final wire state disagrees with the ROM’s canned block on exactly one cell of 32 — cell 0x00, the PRE DELAY — in all three captures. ROOM REVERB 1: canned 33 568 (800 samples, 18.14 ms), wire 33 299. CONCERT REVERB 1 and cold boot: canned 33 268 (500 samples, 11.34 ms), wire 33 255. Both wire values decode to round milliseconds through the firmware’s own evaluator (base 32 770 + round(ms × 44.1) gives 12 ms and 11 ms) while the canned values are round samples. MEASURED Measured against the buffer floor the live taps are 531 samples = 12.04 ms and 487 samples = 11.04 ms. Every “800 samples” here, including on the flowchart, is the canned value.

5.5 The decay coefficient — the one fully reverse-engineered part of the tank

The loop gain is coefficient cell 0x97, consumed at w17, and the firmware computes it: REVERB TIME (s) → level-2 opcode 0x75 → an evaluator at ROM 0x039D98 (constants 0x012E07–0x012F03, a soft-float pow at 0x03D533, a negation at 0x03D404) → a writer at 0x0387E6 that pokes tag 0x26. The law is

cell = int( -( 10 ** ( -4.816 * K / T ) ) / 2 * 2**23 )      =>      chip gain = 2 * cell / 2**23

with K a per-preset constant. Solving T back from each preset’s shipped K and cell lands on the discrete front-panel list for 12 of 12 presets, none off by more than 2 %. The wire confirms it to the bit: the cold-boot poke 0A 74 7B 89 A6 decodes to 0xE8F713 = −0.17996, exactly CONCERT REVERB 1 at REVERB TIME = 2.000 s — one hit in 1600 candidates. MEASURED and proven by construction.

A measured sign disagreement between boot and runtime, inside one capture. The canned bulk load writes cell 0x97 positive (+0.200 ROOM 1, +0.180 CONCERT 1, matching the ROM bank exactly); a later transfer in the same capture pokes it negative. Whether the tank runs on a positive or a negative loop gain therefore depends on whether any parameter has been refreshed since boot. MEASURED Magnitudes can differ too: ROOM REVERB 1’s final runtime value is −0.171802, solving back to REVERB TIME = 0.6000 s at 0.000 % error — matching the screen-verified capture — while its canned cell solves to 0.6995 s, one step up the discrete list at 0.70 s.

No T60 is established and none should be quoted. The gain is known to the bit; the loop it closes is not, because the stage transfer function is open. The single-comb identity T60 = 3D/(−log10 g) is refuted by the ROM directly: DARK REVERB 1, DARK REVERB 2 and BRIGHT REVERB 1 carry the same K over tanks differing by 25 % (13 909 / 12 909 / 10 709 samples), so K is not a loop length. MEASURED

An independent ROM-side witness for the 44.1 kHz frame clock. The firmware’s only delay-descriptor evaluator (opcode 0x67) computes cell = K + ms × 0xAC44 / 0x3E8 = K + ms × 44100/1000, and its UI unit is “ms” in 38 of 38 named sites. MEASURED That is a host-firmware statement of the DSP’s sample rate obtained without any emulator frame counter — stronger evidence for 44 100 Hz than the emulation-side measurement usually cited.

Q0.23 or Q1.22? The bank is not demonstrably read at one uniform scale and the two readings differ by exactly a factor of two. At Q0.23, 0 of 108 diffuser gains (12 presets × 9 stages) reach |g| ≥ 1; at Q1.22, 85 of 108 do, and a one-multiplier all-pass or single-multiply comb with |g| ≥ 1 is unstable. But the decay cell must be Q1.22 because the firmware explicitly halves the gain before storing it — and if every cell is Q1.22 the law reproduces the designer’s gain exactly with no non-uniformity at all. The shipped emulator applies one global 22-bit shift, which makes ROOM REVERB 1’s first diffuser gain 1.50. The stability argument cannot settle it, because the transfer function that would make |g| ≥ 1 unstable is itself undecoded. INFERRED either way. A previously proposed resolution — that hi12 bit 12 selects the coefficient format — is false as stated: word bit 12 is addr8 bit 0, the LSB of the signed pointer displacement, and it is set in 5 of the reverb’s 33 class-A words (w2, w3, w4, w117, w127 — displacements 0x81, 0xFD, 0xF9, 0xF5, 0xFF, all odd). The bit is confounded with the displacement. MEASURED
Four cells the firmware writes and this program never reads. The bank is 37 cells and the cursor stops at 0xB0. There is no cursor reset and no second fetch form anywhere in the image, so 0xB1…0xB4 are written by the host and consumed by nothing here — while 0xB1/0xB2 are ER.LEVEL targets and 0xB2/0xB3/0xB4 are among the cells a preset change moves. Either the base/cursor model is incomplete or the firmware maintains cells the reverb does not use. MEASURED (the observation); unexplained. Relatedly, what the base 0x90 means is the project’s own standing blocker: the reverb’s 33 class-A words resolve at 0x90 (0 of 33 at base 0x00, 33 of 33 at 0x90, in 12 of 12 algorithms), yet the effect-independent kernel header reads the same range as if it were its own coefficients. Three readings survive and no falsifier separates them.

6.1 The failure chain

Word w5 (emulator slot iw205) loads a data-RAM cell into the accumulator. The cell is D-RAM[0x85] — FORCED, because the operand is fetched at the pointer before the word’s own post-increment and the preceding four words’ displacements sum to exactly −256, one full data-RAM wrap, deliberately returning the pointer to base+0. (An earlier note said 0xD0; that was an address error and is retracted.) Nothing deposits a non-zero value in that cell: MEASURED for the content (three arms of the census), proven by construction for the static writer map, and INFERRED that no un-modelled route fills it — the map does not cover the delay-publish path, the input-latch deposit, or any escape word whose data pointer wanders. The host writes the cell exactly once, at boot, with zero. The instrument is not blind: it names a writer the instant an experimental arm creates one.

Do not quote the log line “85: 0/1106751” as “the cell was never written”. That counter block sits outside the store guard — it is a visit counter — and what it prints is “of 1 106 751 visits, 0 carried a non-zero datum”. The no-producer result rests on the falsifier arm and the writer map, not on that line.

With the accumulator dead at w5, 128 of the body’s 133 slots are dead. The five survivors (w0–w4) carry two frame-invariant constants, identical in both buckets; the constants are MEASURED but their attribution to the kernel is INFERRED, as the census has no provenance column. The body’s only link to the rest of the machine is its last word, w132, which stores to a register the epilogue reads — and that link carries zero on 540 000 of 540 000 settled frames. MEASURED

That 540 000 is a pre-correction, 48 kHz-clock window (226 040 quiet + 313 960 loud). The ratio — 100 % — crosses the correction; the wall-clock time does not, and no 44 100 Hz re-measurement of that null exists. Two other windows circulate in this project’s notes (826 040 + 313 960 and 706 040 + 313 960); they are different populations from different instruments and their counts must never be compared with this one.

The null is wider than one cell. In the same run every high data-RAM cell the reverb touches reports zero non-zero values over millions of visits — 85, 87, 88, 89, 8A, 8B, 8C, 8D, 8F, 94, D0, D1, D2 — while cells 0x91 and 0x92 do carry content, proving the instrument can see content when there is any. Unit 1’s entire working set is empty, not one cell of it. MEASURED One tempting explanation is refuted: the null is not an artefact of a zero effect-send — in an arm where unit 0 ran its whole ladder on live audio and fed unit 1, the unit-1 output stage stayed null on every instrument. MEASURED

6.2 What is fixed, and what is not

Eight gated fixes across eleven register sections ship in the device. Those touching the reverb: the per-unit descriptor ring and its pre-increment; the body index fix, which is what gave the delay lines any length at all; the rotation-sign fix, covering all 28 delay accesses; the C-format cursor-consumption fix, which exists because of the reverb (28 non-C-format + 4 C-format = 32 = the number of descriptor cells); a narrowing of the nop guard; two mask moves; and the 44 100 Hz clock. MEASURED

Two fixes commonly assumed to be on this path are not. The store-probe gate wraps only an accounting block, and independently the reverb image contains 0 of 133 words matching its predicate (class-A with the store action) though it has 33 class-A words and 9 such actions. MEASURED The LFO wrap fix has published strength 6 of 11, not 11 of 11, and whether it reaches unit 1 is INFERRED rather than measured — because tap modulation demonstrably does fire on unit 1: 4 323 564 firings with ±240-sample excursions on twelve descriptor cells inside unit 1’s ring (00 01 05 0A 0D 10 16 19 1D 20 23 25). Whether that modulation and wrap arithmetic are faithful there is unmeasured. The modulus fix is not among the eight gates and appears in no shipped channel; whether it reaches unit 1 is likewise unmeasured.

One genuine consistency win, with its caveat: descriptor cell 0x00 now measures a maximum age of 500 frames = 11.34 ms, exactly the canned pre-delay of the cold-boot preset. MEASURED But both sides read the same host parameter stream, so this is an end-to-end check on our own chain — poke decode, doubling rule, descriptor ring, pre-increment, rotation, stamping — and not evidence about the chip (INFERRED). It is still non-trivial: one revision earlier every line measured exactly zero length. Per section 5.3 the live cell 0x00 is 485/487 samples, so the match is against the canned descriptor.

Remaining defects: an output clip rate of 4.924 % (quiet) / 4.920 % (loud) exists with the input exactly zero and is preset-dependent (+1.720 full scale on CONCERT 1, +1.320 on ROOM 1), so it lies inside the reverb’s own parameter path — unexplained MEASURED. The delay-age instrument reports a floor of zero for eight unit-1 cells, so “unit-1 cells span 5.60 ms to 737 ms” mixes one cell’s floor with another’s ceiling. And one shipped fix is described in the register as “not shipped” while the device header enables it by default.

6.3 One clean result, and a trap for when the silence is fixed

A separate defect class in this device fabricates zero operands for unread source codes. The reverb is clean of it, structurally: the image contains zero words carrying any of the six unread source codes and zero words in the collapsed action family. The census reads 0 of 119 863 800 for the reverb body, against 8.772 % for the unit-0 body and 28.492 % for the epilogue. MEASURED (The unit-0 figure sits on a polluted denominator — I-RAM 154…199 is never uploaded, so every unit-0 census in this project carries a 46-slot tail nobody has declared. The reverb has no such tail: 133 slots, 133 words.)

When output does appear, do not call it audio on the strength of a difference from silence. A reverb fed a constant emits a constant. The rule adopted after being caught by exactly that: report the mean and the AC amplitude separately, and beat the 2.2× loud-versus-quiet AC ratio that a known non-audio artefact already achieves. Comparison must be against the input, never against silence.

8.2 What a recording of the real instrument would decide

An impulse response — or a click plus a sustained tone recorded from the direct output with a known preset and known panel settings — is the highest-value missing item in this investigation. It was requested nine days before this document was written and has not been supplied. Precisely what it decides:

The factor-of-two payload doubling (question 7, and the whole millisecond axis)
The first early reflection arrives at one of two times differing by exactly 2×. Timing the first discrete echo after the pre-delay settles it in one take: for ROOM REVERB 1 the candidates are 24.6 ms and 12.3 ms apart and are not confusable.
The per-pass loop gain, hence the coefficient scale (question 7)
Measure the tail’s decay rate against the two readings of C[0x97]. This project guessed the quantity three times and failed three ways — silence, saturation, saturation — because all three guesses read a memory region no algorithm writes. A measured decay makes it a calculation.
Which of the two surviving ladder role assignments is real (question 1)
They are numerically indistinguishable on the coefficients (maximum error 0 over all twelve banks) and differ only in what is written back to the delay line — that is, only in the tail after the first pass. Corpus evidence ranks one first; a tail measurement would confirm or refute it. It is the only known discriminator that is not a further corpus argument.
The direction polarity (question 6)
If inverted, the measured echo pattern will not match the predicted one under any scaling, because the predicted delays go negative.
The currently pre-registered capture cannot test what it claims. It pre-registers “254 / 870 / 978 / 366 / 1044 samples” as CONCERT REVERB 1’s early reflections. Three things are wrong: those numbers are ROOM REVERB 1’s; they are ladder hops, not early reflections; and three of the five are off by one because they were produced by doubling an already-rounded half (255→254, 869→870, 979→978 — exactly the three odd values). Worse, the hop table itself has been retracted in favour of the eleven segments. A capture aimed at CONCERT REVERB 1 must be sized against CONCERT REVERB 1: ladder total 8815 samples, longest single line 1674, live pre-delay 485 samples (canned 500), early reflections at 1866 and 1558 samples = 42.31 and 35.33 ms. ROOM REVERB 1’s two decidable early reflections are 650 and 540 samples = 14.74 and 12.25 ms. MEASURED
An impulse response cannot yet be compared against the emulator, because the emulator emits digital silence. Today it grades the ROM-side model and supplies the missing loop gain; it becomes a regression test only once there is any output at all.

9. What the review changed

This document was written from four independent extractions and then adversarially checked. What the checks changed is listed here, because the value of a page like this lies in what survived challenge rather than in what was asserted.

Dropped as refuted. “The reverb image is only 24.1 % undecoded, so calling it a large fraction overstates it” — on the predicate the headline uses it is 37.6 % decoded, below the corpus mean. “The framing of w5 as the word that kills 128 slots was retracted” — a different sentence was retracted; the messenger framing used here is the retained one. “The published gains 1.200/1.000/1.000 are twice the real values” — they are an accurate quote at Q1.22, and the same extraction destroyed the only published argument for Q0.23; replaced by the open Q question. “The default reverb time disagrees with the screen-verified capture” — reversed: the runtime poke matches at 0.000 % error; the canned bank is the outlier. “Replace the 540 000-frame window with 1 140 000” — that mixed two instruments’ populations and two clocks. Two results built on the retracted ladder table (a 5863-sample “arena”; a tiling contradiction in WAVE REVERB 1) — both double-counting; the forced pairing gives 3873 samples. “1178/3057 is a brief error” — it is the canonical published figure; the real requirement is to name the predicate, which section 2.2 does.

Downgraded. The non-uniform coefficient scale, the flat 16-bit ring, the 0x60-is-write polarity, the LFO-wrap exclusion and every unmeasured cell role: MEASURED or FORCED → INFERRED. “Nothing writes D-RAM[0x85]” became three grades, with INFERRED that no un-modelled route fills it (the host does write it once, with zero). The DRAM’s organisation → INFERRED (part number measured, no datasheet). “14 lines totalling 5863 samples” → twelve lines plus two extra pre-delay taps, 800 + 3873 samples occupied. “Only an impulse response separates the two role assignments” → corpus evidence already ranks one first. “Uniquely among this project’s nulls” → three of five regions read 0.000 %. A gain-versus-filter split by roundness was replaced by a packet-level split by which path last writes the cell.

Added by the review. Two differing banks, not one (29 of 32 descriptor cells differ); the live pre-delay is not the canned pre-delay; the cold-boot preset confirmed independently from the descriptor bank; ten coefficient cells re-poked per preset step, not two; the firmware’s own 44100/1000 constant as a ROM-side witness for the frame clock; the decay coefficient, its evaluator and its law, which one extraction had listed as an unsolved unknown; the routing guard as the dominant decode blocker (1139 words, 60.6 %); tap modulation on twelve unit-1 descriptor cells; the bounding of ACT 0x0B and the minimal pair that would settle it; the corrected front-panel effect-list indices, which showed that one of the anchors used to validate this work was itself off by one; and the defect in the pre-registered hardware capture.

9.1 Errors found in the brief this document was written from

  1. “hi12 bit 10 = END” is unconditional in the brief and conditional in the decoder: END is meaningful only outside the format escape (corpus 121 raw versus 52 real). The same trap applies to the store bit (708 raw, 704 real).
  2. “Decode coverage is 1178/3057 = 38.5 %” is correct but ambiguous — it is the ALU predicate; the tier-1 predicate gives 1247/3057 = 40.8 %, and both are published side by side.
  3. “TYPE index 36 = a99” is off by one: a99 is TYPE 37, a98 is TYPE 36. The effect list has 38 entries; the published type map has 36 rows and is short by two.
  4. “The five streams {79, 88, 89, 90, 91} are for a different chip” is right but incomplete: the partition is 91 to IC311 and nine to IC310 (57, 58, 59, 60, 79, 88, 89, 90, 91), and the standing claim that algorithms 57–60 drive both chips is false.
  5. “They differ by their parameter bank” should read banks, plural.
  6. The presets are selected from the front-panel DIGITAL REVERB page, a fourteen-entry ROM table; naming an emulator automation script as the selection mechanism describes our tooling, not the machine.
  7. “540 000 of 540 000” is correct but is a pre-correction 48 kHz window and should be quoted as a ratio.
