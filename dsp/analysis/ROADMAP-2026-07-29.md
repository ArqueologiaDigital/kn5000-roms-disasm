# 1. WHERE WE ARE

Canonical population: **3057 words** = 38 distinct body images (2974) + resident kernel (60 header + 23 epilogue). Per-image, not per-algorithm. IC311 owns **91** programs; the five formerly-"MALFORMED" streams {79,88,89,90,91} plus algos 57–60 belong to **IC310 (MN19413)** — every `/95`, `/96`, `/100` denominator in the older notes is void.

## 1.2 MEASURED / FORCED — the load-bearing facts that hold

- Per-unit C-RAM coefficient base **`0x00` / `0x90`** (`cram-unit-base.md` item A): unit-1 reverbs resolve 0/33 at base `0x00`, **33/33 at `0x90`**, in 12/12 algorithms; rival "always add 0x90" rejected 79/79 on unit 0.

## 1.4 THE BLOCKER, precisely

**It is not an ISA question.** The ISA audit's top lever (`SRC 0x00`, +348 executable words) is a decode-coverage win that cannot move the audio, because the audio is broken *upstream of the ALU* by four compounding defects, three of which are confirmed by construction:

**(a) INGRESS — the host poke port is unimplemented.** `upd6383.cpp:588/598` treats `cmd 0x01` address `0x0160` as I-RAM word index 352, where nothing executes. Consequence: **all 881 tag-`0x15` D-RAM writes and all 870 tag-`0x4C` descriptor writes are dropped**. Every delay descriptor reads `0x0000`; no per-effect parameter (delay time, reverb time, feedback, high-damp, LFO waveform table, ER level) has ever reached the chip; D-RAM is never zero-filled, so state cells never clear.
*Correction to the source drafts, VERIFIED-HERE:* cmd `0x02` **is** handled (`upd6383.cpp:552`, lands at the `801.0.NN.821` pointer, auto-incrementing) and C-RAM **is** populated — §44's dump is real. The gap is D-RAM and the descriptor bank, not C-RAM.

This manufactured two "chip facts" that were then reasoned from: §42 ("the host's only write path reaches C-RAM; there is no host write to D-RAM anywhere") and §47 ("nothing writes those D-RAM cells") — the latter motivated mask bit 9, which §52 then refuted. Both are **emulator artefacts**.

**(b) OBSERVABILITY — unit 1 has never reached a pin.** VERIFIED-HERE by grep: `m_accb` is assigned **only** at `upd6383.cpp:1849`, behind `m_specmask & 1` — **clear** in the shipped `0x54c`. It is **read** at `:1084` behind `m_speculative` alone, and at `:1536` behind `m_specmask & 2` — also clear. So `w78` presents an accumulator no code path ever writes, and **DO2 is identically 0 on every frame**. The reverbs are the corpus's only unit-1 programs. *Every measurement in §43–§52 — the DC constant, the saturation, "1 560 000 non-zero presentations" — is unit-0/DO1-only and may not be quoted as chip-wide.*

**(c) TIME BASE — the frame clock is 8.8 % wrong.** `kn5000_tonegen.cpp` allocates the stream at **48 000** and hard-codes `SAMPLE_RATE = 48000.0` (lines 78/741/900/1165), against Fs = 44 100 proven four ways. Every emulated delay and reverb time is +8.8 %, which is large enough to be misread as a decode error in any hardware A/B.

**(d) SPACE COLLAPSE — descriptors are aliased onto D-RAM** (`upd6383.cpp:1305`), although D-RAM `0x1D..0x40` (the 36-entry LFO SINE table) overlaps descriptor cells `0x26..0x39`. Harmless only because (a) means neither space is written.

**Downstream state:** the shipping output is a **DC constant** — §46 measured mean 7 492 in *every* window including four seconds before any note, with **no zero crossings**. It has **not been re-measured against a silence window since §52/§53**. Under method rule 13 its status is *presumed DC*, not unknown.

## 1.6 One new, verified structural fact that changes the plan's shape

**PARAMETRIC EQ (algo 39) is a TEN-SECTION, FIVE-BAND STEREO CASCADE, and every open ALU code in the image sits outside every section.** VERIFIED-HERE against `kn5000_subprogram_v142.rom` via `lfo_ramp.algo_to_image()[39]` and `dsp_disasm`:

- The 9-word biquad motif matches **fully** at offsets `[5, 14, 23, 32, 41, 59, 68, 77, 86]` and **on `lo12` alone** additionally at `95` — **ten sections**, five per channel. This agrees exactly with `k3-pointers.md` item I, which MEASURED PEQ's D-RAM state block as `0x50..0x77`, **40 cells, all zero = 5 bands × 2 channels × 4 Direct-Form-I state words**.
- Its C-RAM ships **31 non-zero cells**: `0x00..0x1D` in **five groups of six**, each group closing with `0x800000`, groups 1–4 carrying `0x400000` at offset 1, plus `0x1E = 0x200000`. Five bands × six coefficients + one master gain. **The two channels share one coefficient bank** — which is exactly what a stereo EQ with per-band settings looks like, and it is a hard, checkable prediction.
- The image's **14** undecoded words are `w000–w003, w050–w056, w058, w103, w104`. Thirteen of the fourteen lie **outside all ten sections**; only `w103` sits inside the tenth (variant) section, which is *why* it is undecoded.

With descriptors loaded (P1.1) model CHORUS and measure **lag as a function of time**, scored against `floor(f × 2²³ / 44100)` — predictable to the sample. - **F** `lag(t)` must oscillate at the predicted rate within **±1 %**, and injecting a wrong constant must be **rejected** (the demonstrated NO). - **Hardware cross-check (H4):** the real KN5000's CHORUS rate is measurable to the sample; that single recording falsifies or confirms the whole ingress→routing→LFO chain end to end.
- **deps** P1.1, P3.3, P4.1.

# 6. RISKS AND UNKNOWABLES

## 6.1 Riskiest assumptions, ranked, each with its detector

1. **The descriptor→address chain may be wrong at the root.** Row 14's cell-pairing, the one-cell-per-frame rotation and the 24→16-bit truncation are all GUESSED; only the rotation has good support (SINGLE DELAY's 501/500, NO OPERATION's 4410 = 100.000 ms). If P4.4 or H1 shows the pairing inverted, **much of Phases 3–4 must be re-run.** Detector: H1 and H4, both cheap and early.
2. **P3.0's one-cursor-two-consumers mechanism is INFERRED**, and it is the plan's load-bearing synthesis. Detector: its own pure-ROM falsifier, 1.5 EU, run **before** anything is built on it.
3. **R1 may be blind for the same reason the 9-word window is.** The `f31 = 0` barrier at `w005` is real, and theorem 4 (amplitude is a product of coefficients) is the specific threat. §1.6 shows the *recorded* blindness mechanism for PEQ is wrong, which is encouragement, not proof. Detector: P0.5 run against theorem 4 and the extended window **before** the 4 EU are spent; and R1 step 2's own "all machines identical" falsifier.
4. **`is_dram()` may be wrong**, and §52's disjointness — graded MEASURED, and the basis for retiring mask bit 9 — rests on it. This is the project grading its own decode as evidence. Detector: P3.4, ROM-only, 1 EU.
5. **The twelve stub effects may not be stubs.** Two independent static routes agree, but both are ours. Detector: H2, one minute of owner time.
6. **The two accumulators may be real and unused by this microcode.** The block diagram fact is PROVEN; §28's implementation failed its own pre-stated criterion and §32's bisection showed the ACCB pair *actively harmful* (it caused the input-latch corruption). 7. **The cold-boot capture may not be representative** of runtime parameter pokes. Detector: P1.1's zero-residue parser re-run against a second capture taken while changing effects.
8. **Changing the tone-generator rate touches shipping audio** for the whole KN5000 model. Detector: P1.3's detune check; fall back to a decoupled DSP clock.
9. **The 91/9 IC311/IC310 partition** carries every denominator in the project and has already falsified `/95`, `/96` and `/100` once. Detector: Phase 6's parse falsifier.

This plan therefore spends its first ~15 EU on instruments, ROM-only reference expansion, and **already-MEASURED** plumbing, and schedules **no new speculative reading** until an instrument exists that can reject it.

# 7. STANDING RULES

Each was paid for by a failure. They bind every step in this plan.

1. **The ship gate.** A step ships only with a stated falsifier, a **demonstrated NO** actually run and recorded, and a null/permutation or no-stimulus control. An instrument without a demonstrated NO does not count as built.
2. **An instrument that cannot fail is worthless.** Six were built here; four more were still live at audit time; a seventh (the static def-use/liveness probe) was killed at calibration and is recorded in R3 so it is never rebuilt.
3. **A difference from silence is not a signal** (rule 13). Compare against the **input**, not against zero, and always measure a window with **no stimulus**. A DC constant differs from silence on every sample. The gate ships its own self-test: an injected DC offset must be rejected.
4. **A test with no signal at its input is not a test** (rule 12). State the stimulus; require a control that demonstrably moves (`0 → 8 388 608`).
5. **Read traces in execution order.** A per-slot maximum is not a time series — this was misread three separate times (§3.9, §3.12, §50).
