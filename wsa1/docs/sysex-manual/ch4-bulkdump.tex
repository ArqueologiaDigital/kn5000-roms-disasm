\chapter{Bulk dump}

Technics' user guide states that \textsc{sysex bulk dump} exchanges the instrument's
internal data as System Exclusive, that the instrument is silent during the transfer, and
that it works on the \textbf{MIDI~1} terminals only. This chapter gives the wire protocol.

\section{What can be dumped}
\estab\ The \textsc{sysex bulk dump} screen offers five categories. Each selects a job
code which selects a dump routine.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
row & category & job & routine \\
\midrule
0 & \textsc{total keyboard}     & 0 & \bytes{0xFB22CD} \\
1 & \textsc{sound}              & 3 & \bytes{0xFB22F6} \\
2 & \textsc{combination}        & 5 & \bytes{0xFB2314} \\
3 & \textsc{system, part \& midi} & 4 & \bytes{0xFB2305} \\
4 & \textsc{sequencer}          & 2 & \bytes{0xFB22E7} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Reproduce with \texttt{notes/sysex-probes/sysex\_bulkdump\_tx.py}.

\section{Frame layout}
\estab\ A data frame is

\begin{center}
\bytes{F0 50 2D 04 \textit{nn} 11 \textit{a2 a1 a0} \textit{l2 l1 l0} \textit{[data\ldots]}
\textit{c} \textit{sum} F7}
\end{center}

\begin{description}
\item[\textit{nn}] \bytes{0x00} or \bytes{0x01}, the unit field.
\item[\textit{a2 a1 a0}] destination address, three bytes, base 128 --- the value is
  $a_2 \cdot 128^2 + a_1 \cdot 128 + a_0$. \estab
\item[\textit{l2 l1 l0}] length in \emph{source} bytes, same base-128 encoding.
\item[\textit{data}] the payload, nibble encoded (below).
\item[\textit{c}] continuation flag: \bytes{0x01} if more frames follow, \bytes{0x00} on
  the last.
\item[\textit{sum}] checksum (below).
\end{description}

\estab\ A frame is filled until its byte count reaches \bytes{0xFC} = 252.

\section{Nibble encoding}
\estab\ Every source byte becomes two message bytes, \textbf{high nibble first}:
\[ b \longrightarrow (b \gg 4),\; (b \,\&\, \mathtt{0x0F}) \]
So source byte \bytes{0xF1} is sent as \bytes{0F 01}, and a dump occupies twice as many
bytes on the wire as the length field states. On receive the two are rejoined as
$((\mathrm{first} \,\&\, \mathtt{0x0F}) \ll 4) \mathbin{|} (\mathrm{second} \,\&\,
\mathtt{0x0F})$. An odd body length is a hard error.

\section{Checksum}
\estab\ Let $S$ be the 8-bit sum of every byte from the \bytes{0x50} identifier through the
continuation flag inclusive. Then
\[ \mathit{sum} = (0 - S) \;\&\; \mathtt{0x7F} \]
The leading \bytes{F0}, the checksum byte itself and the trailing \bytes{F7} are outside
the sum. Equivalently, the sum of everything after \bytes{F0} including the checksum is
$0 \bmod 128$.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!6!white,colframe=yellow!50!black,title=The checksum is
not always verified]
\estab\ On receive the checksum is only checked when the matched product id is one of
\bytes{0x7E}, \bytes{0x2B}, \bytes{0x2C} or \bytes{0x2D}. For any other accepted family
\emph{no checksum check is performed at all}.
\end{tcolorbox}

\section{Handshake}
\estab\ Every dump job begins with a two-step enquiry, each step retried up to three times.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Send \bytes{F0 50 21 04 00 11 F7}; expect \bytes{F0 50 22 04 \textit{nn} 11 F7}.
  On success the reply's three identity bytes are stored.
\item Send \bytes{F0 50 22 04 00 11 F7}; expect \bytes{F0 50 23 7E F7} (ACK). On success
  per-frame acknowledgement is enabled for the rest of the dump.
\end{enumerate}

\estab\ If all three attempts of step~1 fail, the error is cleared and \textbf{the dump
proceeds without the acknowledgement protocol}.

\section{Per-frame acknowledgement and timeouts}
\estab\ With the handshake established, each data frame must be answered with
\bytes{F0 50 23 7E F7}. Anything else causes the same frame to be retransmitted, up to
three attempts, after which the session aborts.

\estab\ Without the handshake no acknowledgement is expected: the sender waits for the
MIDI output ring to drain and then pauses 25 timer ticks --- about 51\,ms at the
instrument's 488.28\,Hz tick --- before the next frame.

\estab\ A reply wait times out after 2500 ticks (about 5.1\,s), or 1000 ticks (about
2.05\,s) during the handshake.

\section{Session control messages}
\estab\ After each category the sender emits \bytes{F0 50 27 7E F7} and waits for the ACK.
After the whole job it emits \bytes{F0 50 28 7E F7}. If three retries have failed it then
emits \bytes{F0 50 29 7E F7}, the abort notification.

\section{Continuation frames}
\estab\ Frames after the first re-open with \bytes{F0 50 7E}, followed by data, the
continuation flag, the checksum and \bytes{F7}.

\section{Receiving}
\estab\ The same protocol runs in reverse. The instrument answers a data frame with
\bytes{F0 50 23 7E F7} for ACK, \bytes{F0 50 24 7E F7} for NAK, and
\bytes{F0 50 2A 7E F7} to report status \bytes{0x16}.

\estab\ Inside a session the byte-level framer is stricter than the general receiver: it
accepts \emph{only} \bytes{0x50} as the identifier, not \bytes{0x7E}.
